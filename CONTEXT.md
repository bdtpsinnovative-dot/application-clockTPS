# Domain glossary

## Project

A piece of assigned work that groups the outcomes expected from one or more
employees. A project contains deliverables directly.

## Deliverable

An outcome or work item inside a project. A deliverable has its own owner,
deadline, priority, status, description, evidence links, and attached files.
It can be edited and submitted for review.

## Submission

The act of moving a deliverable to review after the employee has added the
relevant evidence. A submission can later be approved, returned to active
work, or reopened.

## Legacy task card

A previously visible nested work item under a group. Legacy task cards and
their checklist items are retained for data recovery and possible future use,
but they are not part of the current employee-facing project flow.
